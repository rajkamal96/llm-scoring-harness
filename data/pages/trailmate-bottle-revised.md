# TrailMate 750 ml Insulated Steel Water Bottle, Cold 24 h / Hot 12 h, Leak-Proof Lid

Ice for the whole hike. TrailMate keeps water cold for 24 hours and tea hot for 12 hours in our lab test at 25 °C, so the last sip on the trail is as cold as the first.

## Features

- **Keeps temperature:** double-wall vacuum insulation, tested with ice water at 25 °C room temperature.
- **Leak-proof:** the screw lid has a silicone seal and a one-hand carry loop, so it is safe to throw in a school bag.
- **Fits where you need it:** 7 cm base fits most bike bottle cages and car cup holders.
- **Easy to clean:** wide 6.5 cm mouth fits a standard bottle brush and ice cubes.

## Specifications

- Capacity: 750 ml
- Weight: 380 g empty
- Height: 26 cm; diameter: 7 cm
- Material: 18/8 stainless steel body, BPA-free polypropylene lid, silicone seal
- Colours: black, blue, green

Bottle with lid, cleaning brush, care card. Hand wash recommended; not for the microwave or dishwasher lid. 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects.
