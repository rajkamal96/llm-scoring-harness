# Aurora LED Desk Lamp with Wireless Charging Base, 3 Colour Modes, Dimmable

Work late without tired eyes. The Aurora desk lamp gives even, flicker-free light and charges your phone on the base while you work, so your desk has one less cable on it.

## Why people choose it

- **Easier on the eyes:** flicker-free LED light with three colour modes (warm 3000 K, neutral 4000 K, cool 5000 K) so you can switch from reading to focused work.
- **One less charger:** a 10 W Qi wireless charging pad in the base keeps compatible phones topped up while you work.
- **Right light, right place:** the arm folds and the head tilts 90 degrees, so light lands on the page and not on your screen.
- **Quiet and cool:** the aluminium body stays cool to the touch after 8 hours of use.

## Specifications

- Light output: up to 600 lumens, 5 brightness levels
- Power: 12 W lamp, plus 10 W wireless charging, 24 V adapter included
- Height: 46 cm; base: 18 cm x 14 cm; weight: 1.2 kg
- Cable length: 1.5 m
- Materials: aluminium alloy and ABS plastic

## In the box

Lamp, power adapter, quick-start guide, 2-year limited warranty card.

## Care

Wipe with a dry or slightly damp cloth. Do not place metal objects on the charging pad. Wireless charging works with Qi-compatible phones; thick metal cases can block it.
